## How delivery works

We buy your items from major retailers, such as Walmart and Amazon, and have them delivered straight to your address. Your box may show the retailer's name. Large orders can arrive in more than one box on different days. Delivery dates are estimates.

## Before your order is placed with the retailer

- **Orders waiting for approval** are never charged. If the owner rejects one, or no one approves it, nothing is charged and nothing is sent.
- **Canceling a paid order.** If you cancel before we've placed the order with the retailer, we refund it in full. [CODE: cancel-with-refund for paid orders is not built yet.]
- **Subscriptions** can be paused, skipped or canceled at any time before the next order date, and nothing is charged for orders that are skipped or canceled.

## If something goes wrong with a delivery

Please tell us within **30 days** of the expected delivery date.

| What happened | What you get |
|---|---|
| **Tracking says delivered, but it isn't there** | Please check around your door, mailbox and with neighbors, and give it one day. If it still hasn't turned up, we'll send a replacement or refund it, your choice. For problems over $40, we'll check the carrier's delivery record first. |
| **Late or stuck in transit** (more than 5 days past the expected date) | A replacement or a refund, your choice. |
| **Damaged or leaking** | A replacement or a refund for the damaged items. No photo needed for problems up to $40; above that, please send a photo. |
| **Wrong item** | We'll send the right item. Keep the wrong one; you don't need to send it back. |
| **Item missing from the box** | We'll check whether it's coming in a second box. If not, a replacement or a refund for that item. |
| **The retailer canceled an item or it was out of stock** | We'll try another retailer at no extra cost to you. If we can't, we refund that item and let you know. |
| **Charged the wrong amount or twice** | We refund the difference right away. |

## If you don't want something you received

- **Unopened items:** tell us within **30 days** of delivery and we'll refund most of them once the retailer accepts the return. We'll email you a prepaid return label, or, for items under $15, tell you to keep it.
- **Opened household goods** can't be returned for hygiene and safety reasons unless they're damaged, defective or wrong.
- **If your assistant ordered something you didn't want,** tell us within 30 days. We'll refund unopened items as above, and we may refund opened ones too. We'd also like to know: it usually means a limit needs tightening, and we'll help you do that.

## Refunds

- Refunds go back to the card or bank account you paid with. They usually take **5 to 10 business days** to show up, depending on your bank.
- If you prefer, we can give you credit toward your next order instead. We'll never substitute credit for a refund unless you ask.
- Delivery fees are refunded when the whole order was missing, late, damaged or wrong.

## Fair use

Most problems up to $40 are refunded or resent with no questions asked. Almost no one runs into the limits below, but they keep prices low for everyone:

- For problems over $40, we'll ask for a photo (damage) or check the carrier's delivery record (missing).
- After two missing or damaged claims from one household in 90 days, we'll ask for a photo or other proof on every claim, and may wait for the retailer's answer before refunding.
- We may decline claims or close accounts where there's a clear pattern of abuse.

## Product recalls and safety

If a product you received from us is recalled, we'll try to let you know and help you get the remedy the manufacturer offers. For product safety questions, follow the manufacturer's label.

## Disputes with your bank

Please contact us before disputing a charge with your bank. We can almost always fix it faster, and an open dispute may pause your account until it's resolved.
