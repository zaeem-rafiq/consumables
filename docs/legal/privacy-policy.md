This policy explains what information [COMPANY LEGAL NAME] ("**Domaru**", "**we**") collects when you or an AI assistant acting for you use domaru.app, the Domaru API or the Domaru MCP server, and what we do with it.

**The short version:** we collect what we need to deliver your supplies, charge you correctly and keep your account safe. We share your delivery details with the retailer that delivers your order and your payment details stay with Stripe. **We don't sell your information and we don't use it for advertising.**

## 1. What we collect

**Information you or your assistant give us**

- **Account details:** name, email address (which you also use to sign in), household size, and the name of the account.
- **Connected assistants:** which assistants or apps you've connected, when, and what they did on your account.
- **Delivery details:** the name, street address, and phone number for each delivery address.
- **Your rules:** spending limits, approval rules, substitution preferences and subscription settings.
- **Orders and subscriptions:** what was ordered, when, how often, prices, approvals and cancellations.
- **Messages to us:** anything you send to support@domaru.app, including photos of damaged deliveries.

**Information from Stripe, our payment processor**

When you add a card or bank account, you enter it on a Stripe page. Stripe gives us a reference to it, plus the card brand, the last four digits and the expiry date, or the bank name and the last four digits of the account. **We never see or store full card or bank account numbers.**

**Information from retailers and carriers**

Order confirmations, tracking numbers, delivery status and any problems the retailer reports.

**Information collected automatically**

When the website, API or MCP server is used, our servers record technical information such as IP address, the time of the request, which API call or page was used, the name your assistant or app identifies itself with, and error details. We use this to run the Service, prevent abuse and fix problems.

The website sets only three cookies of its own, all needed to run it:

| Cookie | What it does | How long it lasts |
|---|---|---|
| `domaru_owner` | Keeps the owner signed in | 30 days |
| `domaru_back` | Remembers which page to return you to after Stripe's card page | 1 hour |
| `domaru_admin` | Signs in Domaru's own operators (not customers) | 12 hours |

We don't use advertising cookies, tracking pixels or third-party analytics. When you add a payment method on Stripe's page, Stripe sets its own cookies under its privacy policy.

**What we don't get**

We don't receive your conversations with your assistant. We only receive the requests the assistant sends to Domaru, such as "quote these items" or "create this subscription", and whatever details are included in them.

## 2. How we use it

- To create and secure your account, including emailing sign-in links, checking which assistants you've connected, and enforcing your limits and approvals.
- To quote, charge, place and deliver orders and subscriptions.
- To tell you about your account: approvals needed, shipping, problems, refunds, failed payments, and changes to prices or these policies.
- To recommend how much to order and how often, based on your household size and order history, and to flag when you may be running low.
- To provide support and resolve delivery problems, refunds and disputes.
- To prevent fraud and abuse, and to keep the Service secure.
- To meet legal, tax and accounting obligations.
- To understand, in aggregate, how the Service is used so we can improve it.

We don't use your information to train AI models offered to others, and we don't build advertising profiles. [LAWYER: confirm that is the intent long-term.]

## 3. Who we share it with

We share information only as needed to run the Service:

| Who | What | Why |
|---|---|---|
| **Retailers that deliver your order** (currently Walmart, and Amazon as a backup) | The recipient's name, delivery address, phone number and the items ordered | So they can deliver to you. Their own privacy policies apply to what they do with it. |
| **Stripe** | Payment details you enter on Stripe's page, the amounts charged, and your name, email and address for fraud checks and tax | To process payments and calculate sales tax |
| **Service providers** who host our servers, send our emails and store our data | Whatever is needed to provide their service | They act for us and may use the information only to provide the service to us |
| **The assistant or app you connected** | The information it asks for while connected, such as order status, subscription details and your saved addresses | You chose to connect it. What it does with that information is governed by its own privacy policy. |
| **Authorities or others, when required** | What the law requires | To comply with law, respond to valid legal requests, or protect the safety, rights or property of our customers or Domaru |
| **A buyer or successor** | Account and order information | If Domaru is merged, acquired or sells its assets. We'll notify you, and this policy will continue to apply to your information. |

**We do not sell personal information, and we do not share it for cross-context behavioral advertising.**

## 4. How long we keep it

- We keep account and subscription information while your account is open.
- After you close your account, we delete or de-identify your information within [90] days, except for order, payment and tax records, which we keep for [7] years because tax and accounting law requires it, and anything we need to keep to resolve a dispute or prevent fraud.
- Server logs are kept for up to [90] days.
- Test-mode data may be deleted at any time.

## 5. How we protect it

Sign-in links work only once and expire after 30 minutes. Sign-in and approval links, sessions, and the credentials that connected assistants and developer keys use are stored only in scrambled (hashed) form, so we can't read them back. Connections to the Service are encrypted. Card and bank details stay with Stripe. Access to customer information is limited to the people who need it to run the Service. No system is perfectly secure, and if a breach affects your information, we'll notify you as the law requires.

Keep the email account you sign in with secure, and disconnect any assistant you no longer use on domaru.app. A connected assistant can see your orders and addresses and order within your limits.

## 6. Your choices and rights

Wherever you live in the US, you can ask us to:

- **see** the personal information we hold about you,
- **correct** it,
- **delete** it (subject to the records we must keep, described above),
- **get a copy** in a portable format.

Email support@domaru.app from the address on your account. We'll verify the request comes from the account owner and respond within [45] days. We won't treat you differently for making a request. If you live in a state that gives you the right to appeal our decision, you can reply to our response to appeal.

You can stop non-essential emails at any time with the link in the email. We'll still send messages needed for your orders, such as approval requests, shipping updates and receipts.

## 7. Children

Domaru is for adults. We don't knowingly collect information from children under 13, and you must be 18 or older to have an account. If you believe a child has given us information, contact us and we'll delete it.

## 8. United States only

Domaru serves US addresses only, and we store and process information in the United States.
