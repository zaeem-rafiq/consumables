# Pitch copy

Copy for the landing page, directory listings and outreach. The landing page at `/` uses the same words. Lead with trust; the catalog comes second.

## One line

Household supplies your AI agent keeps stocked, within limits you set.

## The three promises

- **It can't overspend.** You set an ask-me threshold, a per-order limit, a monthly cap and allowed categories. Anything outside them waits for you.
- **It can't approve itself.** Approving a held order or raising a limit needs the owner's key. The agent's key can't do either, so there's nothing to talk it into.
- **It never surprises you.** The quote is the charge. Every order, delivery, skip and price change is in a log you and the agent can both read.

## 30-second pitch (people)

You already trust an assistant with your calendar. Let it handle the toilet paper too. Domaru is a store built for AI agents: your assistant sets up deliveries sized to your household and adjusts them when you're running low. You set the limits once. It can't spend past them, it can't approve its own orders, and you never get charged for something you didn't see first.

## 30-second pitch (teams building personal assistants)

Add "and it keeps the house stocked" to your assistant with one connection. Domaru gives your agent stable SKUs, exact quotes, idempotent writes, a test clock and errors that say what to do next. Spending authority is enforced on our side with an owner key, so your users can let the agent run unattended. We also speak Google's Universal Commerce Protocol and a plain REST API.

## 30-second pitch (short-term rental hosts and property managers)

Every unit needs the same paper towels, trash bags and dish soap, on a schedule. Hand the restocking to an agent and keep control of the money: per-order and monthly limits, approval for anything unusual, and one log of every delivery.

## Directory listing (MCP registries, connector directories)

**Domaru: household supplies on subscription.** Search toilet paper, paper towels, detergent, trash bags and more. Create and manage delivery schedules sized to the household. Spending limits and approvals are set by the account owner and enforced by the store.

## Words to avoid

- "Autonomous purchasing" without the limits next to it. The point is bounded authority.
- "Cheapest." We compete on never running out and never overspending, not on price.
